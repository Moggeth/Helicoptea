# Helicoptea — concept collection, 17 October 2026

Sixteen selected images: three existing aircraft, six new aircraft, four utility attachments, gameplay, environments and playful scenarios. Native PNGs are **1536 × 1024**. Open an image for the full-resolution file.

Created with the **built-in image_gen tool** from the prompt set in [prompts.json](prompts.json). Targeted corrections are in [revision-prompts.json](revision-prompts.json); [generation-record.json](generation-record.json) records selected files and measured generation durations. No metered API workflow was used.

These are visual proposals for discussion and 3D authoring, not implemented features or engineering drawings. [ARTIST-HANDOFF.md](ARTIST-HANDOFF.md) is authoritative for mechanics, scale and known inconsistencies. Generated lettering, slogans, dimensions and ratings are placeholders, not approved branding or specifications.

Source inspected: `e4076c35e5cc1d36aab79557740bb68a31229d47`. Current assembled airframes were captured from front, side, rear and overhead using the project's renderer. Three representative [reference captures](references/) are included. Existing vehicle silhouettes, hooks, cargo physics and miniature household scale informed the concepts. More detailed materials and some shapes are proposed interpretations.

## Selected artwork

### 01 · Gameplay — the existing fleet

The core fantasy: a tiny, serious aircraft makes tea. Use this for scale, warmth, material contrast and the hook–line–bag–liquid relationship, not as an exact fleet turnaround.

[![Gameplay — the existing fleet](01-the-art-of-making-tea.png)](01-the-art-of-making-tea.png)

### 02 · Existing aircraft — Micro

Existing Micro, with a central belly hook and open space between the skids. The hero view guides the proposed finish; use the source model to resolve engine/cowling differences between generated views.

[![Existing aircraft — Micro](02-micro-utility-airframe.png)](02-micro-utility-airframe.png)

### 03 · Existing aircraft — Chinook

Existing Chinook, independently suspended loads and a heavy braking attitude. Three belly hooks and two tandem rotor systems remain the defining gameplay contract.

[![Existing aircraft — Chinook](03-chinook-three-hook-haul.png)](03-chinook-three-hook-haul.png)

### 04 · Existing aircraft — Skycrane

Existing Skycrane, with corrected milk-pump routing kept separate from turbines. Use **v2**; the original is retained only as a superseded draft.

[![Existing aircraft — Skycrane](04-skycrane-milk-system-v2.png)](04-skycrane-milk-system-v2.png)

### 05 · Proposed aircraft — Thimble

Proposed **Thimble**: a compact coaxial scout for shelf gaps. Two counter-rotating rotor planes, a short tail, serviceable battery compartment and one light load.

[![Proposed aircraft — Thimble](05-thimble-coaxial-scout.png)](05-thimble-coaxial-scout.png)

### 06 · Proposed aircraft — Swift

Proposed **Swift**: a courier that changes from rotor-borne hover to wing-borne travel. Use **v2**, with forward-facing propeller disks in airplane mode.

[![Proposed aircraft — Swift](06-swift-tiltrotor-courier-v2.png)](06-swift-tiltrotor-courier-v2.png)

### 07 · Proposed aircraft — Lacewing

Proposed **Lacewing**: an open-frame synchropter crane. The exposed winch, triangulated legs and central load path distinguish it from the enclosed Chinook.

[![Proposed aircraft — Lacewing](07-lacewing-intermeshing-crane.png)](07-lacewing-intermeshing-crane.png)

### 08 · Proposed aircraft — Dandelion

Proposed **Dandelion**: a forward-flying autogyro with a pusher propeller and a rigid tea basket. It requires a runway and forward airspeed; hovering is not its proposed role.

[![Proposed aircraft — Dandelion](08-dandelion-autogyro.png)](08-dandelion-autogyro.png)

### 09 · Proposed aircraft — Kingfisher

Proposed **Kingfisher**: a bird-bath amphibian, with braced floats, a high tractor propeller and an internal cargo cassette. Water takeoff and landing would require new mechanics.

[![Proposed aircraft — Kingfisher](09-kingfisher-amphibian.png)](09-kingfisher-amphibian.png)

### 10 · Proposed aircraft — Teacaddy

Proposed **Teacaddy**: a drift-prone cargo airship for the gentlest deliveries. The cream gondola should be lightweight painted model material; its ceramic-like finish is not a request for a heavy ceramic hull.

[![Proposed aircraft — Teacaddy](10-teacaddy-cargo-airship.png)](10-teacaddy-cargo-airship.png)

### 11 · Proposed equipment — Modular tools

Four proposed capabilities: controlled reel, ferrous-object pickup, water spray and a quick-swap cargo latch. Use **v2**, which removes invented load ratings.

[![Proposed equipment — Modular tools](11-useful-little-abilities-v2.png)](11-useful-little-abilities-v2.png)

### 12 · Proposed environment — Greenhouse

A proposed greenhouse route built from potting benches, hanging plants, twine hoops and a destination teacup. Block out rotor clearance and cargo swing before dressing the foliage.

[![Proposed environment — Greenhouse](12-greenhouse-tea-trails.png)](12-greenhouse-tea-trails.png)

A proposed rainy conservatory: warm refuge inside, wet glass outside, and precision milk delivery on a wicker table. Rain, gusts and the location itself are proposals.

[![Proposed environment — Rainy conservatory](13-rain-on-the-conservatory.png)](13-rain-on-the-conservatory.png)

A proposed moonlit pantry, with shelf passages, strong practical light and a load that swings through turns. Keep the readable route and warm/cool lighting hierarchy.

[![Proposed environment — Moonlit pantry](14-midnight-pantry-run.png)](14-midnight-pantry-run.png)

A proposed paper-boat recovery in the existing bird-bath fantasy, watched by a curious cat. Boat attachment and winching would be new interactions.

## Variants and status

The original Skycrane, Swift and tools images remain beside their v2 replacements for provenance. They are **superseded drafts**, not alternative approved mechanisms. The selected-image table always points to the reviewed version.

All sixteen selected images were visually inspected. Revisions corrected the clearest important contradictions. Remaining small view disagreements and unresolved engineering questions are documented in the artist handoff. No game code, production deployment, 3D commission or gameplay balance change is part of this collection.
